Stories Passed Through Generations

Where the Old Legends Live

Philippine folklore reflects the country's many cultures, languages, and local traditions. Stories about spirits and mysterious beings were shared to explain the unknown, teach lessons, and remind people to respect nature.

There is no single version of every creature. Details differ by region, storyteller, and family tradition.
